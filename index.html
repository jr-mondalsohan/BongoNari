Our story

Bengal’s women.
Heritage in every detail.

Bongonari was born from a deep reverence for the timeless grace, strength, and artistry of Bengal’s women. The name itself—Bongo (Bengal) and Nari (Woman)—is a tribute to the modern woman who carries heritage in her heart while boldly shaping her own path.

Our journey began in traditional workshops, where generations of master artisans (karigars) transformed fine metals into delicate art. Every swoop of filigree, every intricate motif, and every carefully set stone reflects a legacy of culture, celebration, and emotion.

At Bongonari, we believe jewelry is more than an ornament—it is a story of pride, identity, and personal empowerment. Whether it’s an heirloom-inspired statement piece for a grand occasion or a minimalist design for everyday elegance, Bongonari is crafted to honor the extraordinary woman in you.
